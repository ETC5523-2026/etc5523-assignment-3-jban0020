# ETC5523 Assignment 3 - Communicating with Data

This repository contains my personal website and blog created for ETC5523: Communicating with Data at Monash University.

**Author:** Joshua Bannon

## Blog Post

**Title:** Could Vaccination Alone Keep COVID-19 Out? What the Delta Modelling Revealed

**Intended audience:** Australian adults interested in public-health decisions who do not have a specialist background in epidemiology or statistical modelling.

The blog explores research on COVID-19 border quarantine, the Delta variant and vaccination, focusing on how different levels of vaccine efficacy and community vaccination coverage influence the risk of outbreaks.
